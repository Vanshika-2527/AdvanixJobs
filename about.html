Smart JobConnect

About Smart JobConnect

AI-powered unified job and internship platform.

Our Mission

Finding the right internship or first job shouldn't be complicated. Smart JobConnect brings students and recruiters together on one platform where opportunities are easy to discover, skills are matched transparently, and every application is tracked.

We built this platform to help college students showcase their skills, calculate their job-readiness, and connect with employers who value potential over pedigree.

Core values

  • Student-first: Features designed around student needs.
  • Transparency: Skill match is calculated openly, not hidden in a black box.
  • Accessibility: Simple, clean, and works on any device.
  • Integrity: No scraping, no fake integrations — clearly marked demo data.

Who It Is For

S

Students

Discover internships & jobs, save favorites, track applications, and see skill-match percentages.

R

Recruiters

Post jobs, manage listings, review applicants, and shortlist or reject candidates.

Tech Stack (Phase 1)

  • HTML5 · CSS3 · Vanilla JavaScript
  • LocalStorage for prototype persistence
  • Font Awesome icons · Google Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter)
  • Designed for future Node.js / Express / MongoDB integration

Built for Learning & Portfolios

This prototype is designed as a college project and resume/portfolio piece. It demonstrates a complete frontend job-platform flow with simulated authentication, LocalStorage persistence, and a rule-based job matching system — ready to evolve into a full-stack application.

Demo data is used throughout. Not production-secure.
